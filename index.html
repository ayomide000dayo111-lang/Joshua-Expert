Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How much does a project cost?

Pricing starts from $200 for audits, fixes, and single-page work. Full store builds typically start around $900, and ongoing care plans start from $150/month. Every quote depends on scope, so the exact number comes after a short call.

How long does a typical build take?

Small fixes and single pages usually take 3–5 days. A full store build takes 2–4 weeks depending on the number of products, integrations, and how quickly content is provided.

Which platforms do you work with?

Shopify, WooCommerce, Webflow, and Wix. If you're not sure which platform fits your business, that's part of the first conversation.

Can you migrate my existing store without losing my SEO?

Yes — migrations are planned around preserving your URL structure, redirects, and order history so your search rankings carry over.

Do you offer support after launch?

Every build includes launch support, and ongoing maintenance is available as a monthly plan if you want updates and monitoring handled for you.
